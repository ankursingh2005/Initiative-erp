Incentive Project

Initiative ERP | Monthly sales and incentive reporting

Upload monthly outlet sales and review the calculated incentive by outlet, category and reporting group.

Basic calculation

Estimated average profit = Total sales × Profit rate
Base incentive = Estimated average profit × Incentive rate

The default profit rate is 7% and the default incentive rate is 2.5%. Both are configurable. These are estimated incentive inputs, rather than actual product-cost profit.

Additional report calculations

Exact sales incentive: the calculated base incentive is multiplied by the applied share percentage configured for the outlet / group.

IDS Fund: a separate calculation uses sales multiplied by the configured outlet / category incentive rate, then applies the fund share. Non-sales reporting uses this fund allocation. It does not use the basic 7% / 2.5% formula for every total.

User workflow

  1. Open Monthly Sales & Incentive Report.
  2. Select an Excel workbook (.xlsx / .xlsm) or a readable text-based PDF.
  3. Check the selected rates and upload the file.
  4. Review outlet totals, category breakdowns and the available sales / non-sales reports.
  5. Select SALES, NON SALES or BOTH, then download Excel or PDF.
Review before using the report
Confirm the sales period, outlet names, categories and rate settings. Missing rate or fund configuration can leave totals pending. The basic calculated summary and the exact / fund reports may show different totals because they apply different rules.
