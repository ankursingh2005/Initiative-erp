# Attendance - Illustrated User Guide

Simple instructions for Initiative ERP. Based on project source reviewed on 17 September 2026. Illustrations are teaching examples, not live employee records.

## 1. Sign in and open Attendance

Attendance records your punch-in and punch-out time, selfie, and location. You can check your history and download your monthly attendance.

### Get ready

- Keep your username or email and password ready.
- Use a phone or computer with internet, a camera, and location access.
- Turn on GPS and allow precise location where your device offers it.

### Open the page

1. Open your browser and visit **https://erp.initiative.co.in**.
2. Enter **Username / Email** and **Password**, then tap **Sign in**.
3. From Home, open the navigation menu if needed and choose **Attendance**.
4. Check your name, today's date, and the outlet shown on the page.

**You should see:** **My Attendance**, **Leave & Weekly Off**, today's record, and **Attendance Summary**. Admin, Owner, and HR accounts also have an attendance dashboard.

Direct page: `https://erp.initiative.co.in/attendance`.

**Tap** means touch once on a phone. **Click** means select with a mouse. If you forgot your password, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. Ask your administrator if you need an account or your outlet is wrong.

## 2. Allow location and camera

The app needs location and a fresh selfie for both punch in and punch out. Opening the camera alone does not save attendance.

### Check your permissions

1. Open the secure website using **https://**.
2. Allow **Location** when your browser asks.
3. Allow **Camera** when you open the punch action.
4. Wait for the location check and camera preview to be ready.
5. If GPS is inaccurate, move to an open area near your outlet and retry.

### Where can you mark attendance?

| Account type | Location rule |
| --- | --- |
| Most employees | Be inside your assigned outlet's allowed area. The maximum is 100 metres; a smaller configured radius may apply. |
| Service Manager, AC Technician A, AC Technician B, HR | May mark from any location. GPS and selfie are still required; the nearest available outlet is used as a reference. |

The app records the server's current India time. Changing your phone clock does not change the saved punch time.

**Before continuing:** check that your face is visible, your internet works, and the displayed account belongs to you.

## 3. Punch in at the start of work

### Record your arrival

1. Under **Leave & Weekly Off**, check that today's status is **Working**.
2. Go to the **Today** panel and tap **Punch In**.
3. Wait while the app prepares location and camera.
4. Face the camera and check the preview.
5. Tap **Capture & Punch In** once when the button becomes available.
6. Wait while the page says **Saving attendance...**.
7. Check the saved punch-in time in today's record.

**You should see:** your punch-in time and an available **Punch Out** button. Punch In is hidden after the saved record loads.

### Make sure it was saved

- If the app says **Attendance saved. Refresh to load the latest record.**, refresh and check the record.
- If a connection error appears, refresh and check your history before trying again. The server may already have saved the punch.
- If there is no saved punch, reopen the action and take a fresh selfie.
- Use **Cancel** to close the camera without submitting.

The current system accepts one punch in and one punch out for an account on a date. It does not provide repeated in/out sessions for breaks.

## 4. Punch out at the end of work

### Record your departure

1. Open **Attendance** before leaving the allowed outlet area.
2. Check that today's punch-in record is present.
3. Tap **Punch Out**.
4. Wait for location verification and the camera preview.
5. Look at the camera and tap **Capture & Punch Out** once.
6. Wait for saving to finish.
7. Check that both punch times and the work duration appear.

**You should see:** **Punch in**, **Punch out**, and the hours worked. The Punch Out button disappears once the saved record loads.

### Understand work hours

The displayed duration is the time between punch in and punch out. While today's session is open, the page shows an ongoing duration. This is not a calculation of approved pay or deducted breaks.

**Example only:** punch in at 10:00 and punch out at 18:30 gives **08:30 hours** between the two punches.

If you forgot to punch out on a previous day, the history can show **Punch out missing**. Contact your administrator; a new punch today does not repair yesterday's record.

## 5. Set leave and weekly off

These controls are in **Leave & Weekly Off**. They can be used from anywhere without a selfie or location check.

### Mark Leave for today

1. Find **Today's status**.
2. Change **Working** to **Leave**.
3. Wait for the page to reload after saving.
4. Check that the status remains **Leave**.

While Leave is selected, attendance marking is disabled. If you are working today, change it back to **Working** before punching in.

You cannot change today to Leave after attendance has already been marked. This control records today's status; it is not a future-date leave application or an approval workflow.

### Choose your weekly off day

1. Find **Weekly off day**.
2. Select the weekday agreed with your team.
3. Wait for the saved indication and check the selected day.

Weekly off repeats on the selected weekday. It is not a one-day leave selection. If the control is hidden, loading, or cannot be saved, ask your administrator to check your account.

**Remember:** selecting **Working** does not itself mark you Present. You still need to punch in successfully.

## 6. Review your history and selfies

### Open a month's records

1. Scroll to **Attendance Summary**.
2. Choose the month from the month selector.
3. Read **Avg Punch In Time**, **Avg Punch Out Time**, and **Avg Time Spent**.
4. Tap **Previous days** to expand older records in that month.
5. Check each date's **Punch In**, **Punch Out**, and **Time Spent**.

The punch-time averages use available saved times. Average Time Spent can include today's ongoing session. A previous day without punch out is excluded from the duration average.

### View the saved images

1. Tap **View** on the date you want.
2. Check the date, outlet, punch times, and selfie panels.
3. Tap a saved image to enlarge it.
4. Close the enlarged image, then close the attendance details.

**You should see:** separate **Punch In** and **Punch Out** image panels. A missing punch or unavailable image may show a message instead of a photo.

### Download your month

Select the required month, then tap **Monthly Attendance**. Wait for the Excel file to download, open it, and check the month and records. The filename follows **my-attendance-YYYY-MM.xlsx**.

## 7. Use the admin dashboard

This section is for **Admin, Owner, and HR** accounts. Other employees can continue using their own attendance summary.

### Choose the people and date

1. Open **Admin Attendance Dashboard**.
2. Select the required calendar date.
3. Select an outlet and employee category if needed.
4. Leave the **Week Off** filter unselected for a normal daily view, or choose a weekday to inspect that weekly-off group.
5. Read the **Total Users**, **Present**, **Absent**, **Week Off**, and **Leave** cards.
6. Tap a card to show its employees, or use **Show employees**.
7. Use **Search employee by name or email** to find a person.

On a phone, swipe the employee table sideways to see more columns. Tap the total card to return to all statuses, and clear the search when finished.

### Read the status carefully

| Status | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Present | A punch-in record exists; check punch out separately. |
| Absent | No qualifying attendance, leave, or weekly-off status in the selected daily view. |
| Week Off | A weekly-off result; the weekday filter also helps inspect scheduled groups. |
| Leave | Leave is recorded for that date. |

Check filters before interpreting totals. A weekday filter can change the group shown; the cards are not a payroll approval.

## 8. Inspect an employee's record

### Open the details

1. Find the employee in the dashboard table.
2. Tap the employee's row.
3. Check the employee name, date, and outlet.
4. Use the date selector in the popup to view another available attendance record.
5. Review punch in, punch out, working hours, and the saved selfies.
6. Tap an image to enlarge it, then close it when finished.
7. Use **Close** at the top of the popup to return to the dashboard.

### Read location information correctly

Live distance is the latest reported distance from the reference outlet. An **Active** location label depends on recent location updates. **Inactive** can mean updates are no longer arriving; it does not by itself prove absence. **Completed** indicates a finished attendance session in the location display.

Location updates depend on browser and device permissions and whether the page can keep reporting. Do not treat a stale location as a current position.

### Download one employee's history

Inside the employee popup, open **Download** and choose **Excel** or **PDF**. This action exports the employee's full available attendance history, not just the date currently displayed in the popup.

Check the employee and date range after opening the file. The popup's **Present ... / ... days** indicator compares recorded days with calendar days; it is not an approved working-day target.

## 9. Download and share admin reports

### Download the selected day's report

1. Set the calendar date, outlet, employee category, and any required filters.
2. Check the status card selected on the dashboard.
3. Open **Download** and choose **Excel** or **PDF**.
4. Wait for the download, open the file, and verify its date and people.

### Download a monthly workbook

1. Choose the required month in the admin month selector.
2. Review the outlet, category, weekly-off, and status filters.
3. Open **Download** and choose **Monthly Excel**.
4. Open the workbook and check the reporting month and included employees.

The month selector is used for the monthly export; the calendar date controls the daily view. Search text is not an export filter. Clear filters when you need a broader report.

### Send the configured WhatsApp report

**Send WhatsApp** sends an outlet-wise summary for the selected calendar date to recipients configured by your administrator. It does not send the currently searched employee table or use its outlet/category filters.

Only tap it when you intend to send that report. Wait for the success message. If the service or recipients are not configured, ask your administrator to complete the setup.

## 10. Solve common problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Location denied or unavailable | Turn on GPS; allow Location for this site in browser settings. Refresh and retry. |
| Outside the allowed area | Move near the assigned outlet. Ask an administrator if the outlet assignment is wrong. |
| GPS inaccurate or expired | Enable precise location, move to an open area, and reopen the camera action. |
| Camera denied or unavailable | Allow Camera, close other apps using it, and reopen the secure website. |
| Capture button unavailable | Wait for both camera preview and location verification. Read the on-screen message. |
| Saving failed or timed out | Refresh and inspect the record before retrying. If missing, take a fresh selfie. |
| Already recorded | Check today's saved record. Duplicate punches are not accepted. |
| On Leave Today | Switch today's status to Working if appropriate, then punch in. |
| Session expired | Sign in again, then check your record before marking. |
| History or export does not load | Check internet, month, date, and filters. Refresh; contact your administrator if it continues. |

### Your daily checklist

- Confirm the account, date, and outlet before punching in.
- Wait for the saved record after each punch.
- Punch out before leaving the allowed area.
- Check both times and report missing records promptly.

Guide basis: project files **static/attendance.html**, **static/attendance-capture.js**, and attendance routes in **main.py**, reviewed on 17 September 2026. The deployed version may differ.
