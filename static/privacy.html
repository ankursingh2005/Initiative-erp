Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Initiative Data Systems Private Limited ("IDSPL", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information through the Initiative ERP web and mobile application ("the App"). The App is an internal business tool used by IDSPL staff to manage sales, schemes, and purchase orders across our stores. It is not intended for use by members of the public.

Information We Collect

When you use the App, we collect and store:

  • Account information: username, email address, role (e.g. Admin, Category Manager, Accounts), and assigned store/brand/ category, provided when your account is created.
  • Business data you enter: sales records, scheme details, purchase order requests, supplier information, and related documents you upload or submit while using the App.
  • Authentication data: your password, stored only in hashed (encrypted) form — we cannot see or recover your actual password.

We do not collect location data, contacts, camera/microphone access, or any information beyond what you directly enter into the App or what is necessary for it to function.

How We Use Information

  • To operate core App features: authentication, role-based access, sales/scheme tracking, and purchase order processing.
  • To send purchase-order-related emails to suppliers, when you use the "Send PO Email" feature.
  • To let Administrators manage user accounts, including resetting a user's password on request.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

How We Share Information

Information is only visible within IDSPL, scoped by role (for example, a Category Manager sees only their assigned category's data). We share information with outside parties only in these limited cases:

  • With suppliers, when a Purchase Order is emailed to them as part of normal procurement.
  • With our hosting provider (Render) and, if scheme-document extraction is used, with Anthropic's Claude API, solely to operate the App's infrastructure and features — not for their own purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in an encrypted-in-transit (HTTPS) connection to our PostgreSQL database. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt hashing and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

Account Recovery

The App does not send password-reset emails. If you're locked out of your account, contact your Administrator, who can reset your password directly from the App's user management panel.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and business data is retained for as long as your account is active and as needed for IDSPL's business records. To request deletion of your account or personal information, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended solely for use by IDSPL employees and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
